The Quintic Reciprocity Law is used to produce an algorithm, that runs in polynomial time, and that determines the primality of numbers <i>M</i> such that <i>M</i>
<sup>4</sup> − 1 is divisible by a power of 5 which is larger that \(\sqrt{M}\)</span>, provided that a small prime <i>p</i>, <i>p</i> ≡ 1 (<i>mod</i> 5) is given, such that <i>M</i> is not a fifth power modulo <i>p</i>. The same test equations are used for all such <i>M</i>.</p><p>If <i>M</i> is a fifth power modulo <i>p</i>, a sufficient condition that determines the primality of <i>M</i> is given.</p></div></div></section>
